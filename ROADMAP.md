# Capitol-Alpha Roadmap

Congressional financial disclosures and market analysis roadmap.

## v1.1 — Automated Disclosure Scraping

- [ ] **House & Senate PTR Ingestion**: High-reliability ingestion pipeline for periodic transaction reports.
- [ ] **Normalized Asset Entity Linking**: Automated ticker symbol resolution and corporate entity mapping.

## v1.2 — Portfolio Backtesting & Signal Modeling

- [ ] **Committee Trade Correlation Analysis**: Sector-specific trading signal generation based on committee assignments.
- [ ] **Interactive Visual Explorer**: Accessible chart visualizer for member trading histories.

## Out of Scope

- Partisan editorializing or speculative sentiment analysis
- Automated direct stock trading execution
